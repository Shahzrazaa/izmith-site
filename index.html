05 · FAQ

Straight answers.

If your question is not here, email hello@izmith.com and we will reply with a clear next step.

What is the free audit?

A first look. Send a sample file, and we will tell you whether there is wasted spend or catalog mess worth a full job. The paid audit is the full report, cleanup file, and reallocation plan.

What do I need to send?

For a PPC audit, your search-term export. For catalog work, a CSV or Excel of the catalog. For a listing audit, the live listing URLs. If you are unsure, email the file and we will tell you if it is enough.

Do you build online stores?

No. We do not sell store builds. We clean catalogs, audit ads, and set up analytics on the channels you already use.

How do export and local prices work?

Export clients are invoiced in USD at the starting prices listed above. Pakistani clients can be invoiced in PKR at the local starting prices. First paid pilots can be quoted lower.

How fast is delivery?

Listing audits take 2–4 days. PPC audits take 3–5 days. Catalog cleanup takes 3–7 days. An exporter data pack takes 1–2 weeks.

Who does the work?

Shahzad Raza Arain, the founder, scopes and checks every job. Analysis and files are drafted with AI tools, then reviewed before delivery.

Where are you based?

Karachi, Pakistan. IZMITH is registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan. We work with clients locally and abroad.
